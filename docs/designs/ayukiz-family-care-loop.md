## Problem Statement

In many Indian families, one adult informally coordinates healthcare for themselves, children, and ageing parents. Care is split across paper prescriptions, reports, medicines, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, doctor visits, and memory. The family often has no reliable answer to four basic questions: what is the current care plan, what remains unresolved, what happens next, and who owns it.

Ayukiz will start fresh as a three-mode family care coordinator:

1. After a doctor visit, convert the doctor's documented instructions into a verified medicine schedule, dietary restrictions, follow-ups, warning-sign questions, and owned tasks.
2. Before and after a second opinion, assemble the evidence and questions, preserve both doctors' documented opinions, capture the new opinion, and expose decisions that still require a clinician.
3. Between visits, help a remote adult child oversee an elder parent's medicine routine and outstanding care tasks through low-effort WhatsApp check-ins.

The product does not decide which doctor is correct. Its job is to keep the family care state accurate, visible, and moving.

## Demand Evidence

- A friend's father with a cardiac history experienced chest pain. One doctor recommended angioplasty; a second doctor, reviewing the same records, treated the issue as gastric and asked for a one-month follow-up. Before that follow-up, the father experienced chest pain again and was admitted for emergency care. No one had explicitly recorded warning signs, assigned monitoring responsibility, or owned the follow-up. This case demonstrates unresolved clinical uncertainty and weak follow-through, not that either doctor was wrong.
- Another friend repeatedly travels to help manage his father's lung condition. He explicitly said he would pay for a service that could replace some of this back-and-forth travel.
- The same friend consulted two doctors and tried several medicines for leg pain before a skin-related explanation emerged. This demonstrates the practical burden of multi-doctor care, but not that Ayukiz could or should diagnose the condition.
- Existing family behaviour already includes collecting paper prescriptions and lab reports, carrying them to appointments, asking social groups for doctor recommendations, and remembering follow-ups informally.

The strongest demand signal is willingness to pay from a remote adult child already spending time and money on travel. It remains verbal evidence until converted into a prepaid pilot.

## Status Quo

The elder parent usually self-manages day to day. The adult child checks in casually and intervenes when something feels wrong or a visit becomes necessary.

- Records are kept physically or as scattered phone images.
- Doctor instructions are acted on but rarely transformed into a shared care plan.
- Medicine start/stop instructions, restrictions, warning signs, and follow-up dates may remain implicit.
- A second opinion can produce more uncertainty when opinions differ.
- Nobody is explicitly responsible for monitoring, escalation, or closing the loop.
- Remote children compensate through repeated calls, travel, and last-minute intervention.

The real competitor is this family-operated paper, memory, phone-call, and travel workflow.

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

Paying user: a remote adult child who already spends time, travel, or money managing an elder parent's chronic or recurring care.

Participating user: the elder parent, who should be able to confirm simple facts through WhatsApp or voice without maintaining a dashboard.

Initial offer: one elder parent, one remote caregiver, and a 30-day WhatsApp Care Loop Concierge covering all three modes when triggered.

The pilot promise is:

> Know the current care plan, what is unresolved, what happens next, and who owns it.

The promise is not diagnosis, emergency triage, treatment selection, prevention of misdiagnosis, or an autonomous AI doctor.

## Constraints

- A Registered Medical Practitioner, treating clinician, or qualified pharmacist remains accountable for clinical judgment within their professional scope.
- Ayukiz may extract, organize, summarize, compare documented statements, prepare questions, schedule instructions, collect confirmations, and surface unresolved items.
- Ayukiz may not choose between doctors, diagnose conditions, interpret reports for the patient, approve or reject treatment, change medicine, judge dosage safety, or tell a patient that symptoms are safe to wait on.
- Every free-text symptom message receives conservative routing language directing the family to the treating clinician, emergency service, or nearest hospital if urgent. A clinician-approved deterministic keyword list may add a high-priority banner, but Ayukiz never estimates severity or says it is safe to wait.
- Every care item must retain provenance and status. Doctor-authored instructions require a clinician-authored document or clinician-confirmed message plus family confirmation. An operator note based on family recollection remains `family_reported` and cannot be presented as doctor-authored.
- Real health data requires explicit patient consent, restricted non-git storage, purpose limitation, access controls, retention and deletion procedures, and an audit trail.
- The parent interaction must be low effort: WhatsApp, voice, caregiver entry, or simple confirmations. The product cannot depend on the elder maintaining a daily dashboard.
- The initial service is operator-supervised. AI drafts; the operator authorizes every state transition and either sends a message manually or activates a fixed, pre-approved template schedule. No model-generated message is sent without operator approval. No autonomous clinical model or ABDM integration is required to test demand.

### Launch Gates

Only synthetic data may be used until all of these are complete:

1. A restricted pilot stack is selected and documented, including every vendor that can see message or record content.
2. Operator devices use full-disk encryption, screen lock, multifactor authentication, disabled public sharing, and an approved backup policy.
3. Patient consent, caregiver authority, access scopes, revocation, export, retention, deletion, and breach-response procedures are approved through India-focused legal and clinical review.
4. The family verifies its treating-clinician contact, local emergency contact, and preferred escalation order.
5. The operator playbook states service hours, response expectations, suspension criteria, and the explicit warning that Ayukiz is not continuously monitored.
6. The exact WhatsApp operating method is tested with synthetic cases. If fixed-template scheduling and status capture are verified, the operator may activate those templates. Otherwise, messages are manual and offered only within staffed hours without timer guarantees.

The design intentionally does not choose a health-data storage vendor without that review. This is a launch blocker, not work deferred until after real records arrive.

### Consent and Authority

- A patient with decision-making capacity gives explicit, purpose-specific consent before records are processed or messages begin. Capture the consent text, channel, timestamp, intended caregiver, permitted actions, and retention choice.
- The caregiver separately accepts their role and permissions. Pilot scopes are limited to `view care state`, `submit records`, `confirm family-reported facts`, and `receive exceptions`; no caregiver may consent to clinical treatment through Ayukiz.
- If capacity or legal authority is uncertain, Ayukiz suspends setup. The pilot does not decide capacity or recognize a proxy until the required authority has been validated under the approved policy.
- Consent can be revoked through the intake channel. Revocation runs one atomic operator checklist: suspend the case; cancel queued templates, retries, caregiver exceptions, autoresponders, reviewer access, and pending exports; revoke caregiver and operator access not needed for deletion; verify the cancellations in the log; offer the permitted export; and begin the approved deletion procedure. No deletion step proceeds until cancellation verification is recorded.
- Permission changes propagate to the case log and all operator-access lists before further processing.
- A deletion receipt may retain only a non-clinical case identifier, request timestamp, completion timestamp, and operator identity for the approved audit period. It must not retain symptoms, medicines, records, or message content.

## Premises

1. The remote adult child is the payer and coordinator; the elder parent is a low-effort participant.
2. The three functions are event modes over one shared care state, not three disconnected products.
3. Differentiation comes from ownership and follow-through, not generic record storage or medicine reminders.
4. Conflicting medical opinions are recorded as unresolved clinical decisions. AI does not clinically reconcile them.
5. A medical LLM is a copilot for administrative work, not a substitute for a licensed professional.
6. The business should learn the clinical-escalation rate during manual pilots before committing to an in-house reviewer network.

## Landscape Findings

- Informal caregivers already perform medication acquisition, organization, administration, adherence monitoring, and side-effect observation. Reminder-only software captures only a fraction of the job.
- India policy work supports remote monitoring, medication management, reminders, family involvement, and emergency infrastructure, while also identifying elder digital-literacy and fragmented-regulation constraints.
- ABDM-compatible applications already provide consent-based record linking, storage, and sharing. Ayukiz should not compete as a generic record vault.
- Existing products already market WhatsApp reminders, missed-dose visibility, family health logs, and caregiver dashboards.
- Second-opinion research reports wide variation in agreement and shows that disagreement itself can create confusion. The product must manage uncertainty without claiming a correct clinical answer.
- Current medical LLM research supports decision assistance, but practice-based evaluations still show gaps in synthesis, workflow integration, and safety. Software-first delivery does not remove professional accountability.

## Approaches Considered

- **WhatsApp Care Loop Concierge:** chosen because it covers the full product experience while keeping operations manual and elder participation familiar.
- **Family Care OS:** deferred because software does not remove clinical accountability and would require substantial security, permissions, notification, and workflow infrastructure before demand is proven.
- **Managed Family Care Membership:** deferred because the broader service promise would require coordinator staffing, service-level commitments, and more professional coverage before the clinical-escalation rate is known.

## Recommended Approach

Launch the WhatsApp Care Loop Concierge as a 30-day paid pilot.

AI performs administrative drafting aggressively. It extracts documents, drafts care states, generates factual clarification questions, proposes tasks, and drafts caregiver summaries. The operator verifies extraction, activates care items, approves template schedules, and reconciles confirmations into the care state. Only fixed templates from an operator-approved schedule may send automatically. Clinical questions are routed to the family's treating doctor, emergency care, or a qualified paid reviewer when one is explicitly engaged.

### Shared Care State

Each family case maintains:

- Patient identity and consent status.
- Caregiver identity, relationship, contact details, and permissions.
- Known conditions and allergies as reported, including unknown status.
- Source documents with dates, authors, and extraction confidence.
- Documented clinical opinions, each attributed to its doctor and visit.
- Current doctor-authored instructions with start date, source, and family confirmation.
- Medicines, restrictions, follow-ups, and care tasks.
- Unresolved questions and the person responsible for answering them.
- Confirmation events such as medicine taken, follow-up booked, or task missed.
- Escalation state and care contact used.
- Complete change history so the family can see who changed what and why.
- `last_reconciled_at`, count of pending inbound items, and count of unresolved drafts shown on every caregiver-facing summary so "current" never implies that an unprocessed message was included.

This is an operational pilot schema, not yet a permanent application database.

### Canonical Pilot State Model

Only the operator may execute case or care-item transitions. Every transition records actor, timestamp, source state, target state, and reason.

| Entity | From | To | Guard or reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Case | `setup` | `needs_consent` | Family setup started |
| Case | `needs_consent` | `active` | Consent, authority, verified contacts, approved storage, and communication policy complete |
| Case | `active` | `suspended` | Consent concern, unsafe use, nonpayment, clinician withdrawal, unreachable family, or operational failure; reason stored separately |
| Case | `suspended` | `active` | Suspension reason resolved and permissions rechecked |
| Case | `active` or `suspended` | `closed` | Pilot completed or family ends service; final summary and export offered |
| Case | Any non-deleted state | `deletion_pending` | Valid deletion request or mandatory deletion trigger |
| Case | `deletion_pending` | `deleted` | Controlled copies deleted and deletion receipt recorded |

`unsafe_or_unreachable` is a suspension reason, not a case state.

All medicines, restrictions, tests, follow-ups, warning instructions, and general care tasks use one care-item envelope with two classes:

- `clinical_instruction`: medicine, restriction, test, follow-up, or warning instruction attributed to a clinician source.
- `administrative_task`: booking, collecting records, sending a packet, confirming an appointment, or another explicitly non-clinical family coordination action.

- `class` and `type`: clinical instruction types are medicine, restriction, test, follow_up, and warning_instruction; administrative task types are booking, collect_records, send_packet, confirm_appointment, and other_nonclinical.
- `exact_source_text`, `source_type`, `source_author`, `source_date`, `source_location`, and extraction confidence.
- `family_confirmation`, responsible owner, due or schedule information, and last-reconciled timestamp.
- `status`: `draft_extracted`, `needs_confirmation`, `reported_unverified`, `active`, `paused`, `disputed`, `superseded`, `completed`, or `cancelled`.

| Care-item transition | Guard |
|---|---|
| `draft_extracted` -> `needs_confirmation` | Required fields transcribed and source linked |
| `needs_confirmation` -> `active` | For a clinical instruction, family confirms transcription, clinician provenance exists, and no governing contradiction remains; for an administrative task, the family or operator confirms the non-clinical task and owner |
| `reported_unverified` -> `needs_confirmation` | For a clinical instruction, clinician-authored or clinician-confirmed source obtained; for an administrative task, family or operator origin is sufficient and remains visibly non-clinical |
| `needs_confirmation` -> `disputed` | Family rejects the transcription or identifies a source conflict |
| `draft_extracted` or `needs_confirmation` -> `cancelled` | Extraction error, duplicate, or non-applicable draft |
| `active` -> `paused` | Administrative pause requested; never presented as a clinical stop order |
| `active` or `paused` -> `disputed` | A source conflict affects the item |
| `active`, `paused`, or `disputed` -> `superseded` | A newer clinician-authored instruction is sourced and family-confirmed |
| `active` -> `completed` | Finite action or sourced course completes |
| Any nonterminal state -> `cancelled` | Duplicate draft, extraction error, or case closure; clinical discontinuation requires a sourced instruction |

Only `active` care items may generate routine prompts. Disputed items generate a clinician question, never a temporary AI-selected plan. `superseded`, `completed`, and `cancelled` are terminal for that version; a new version creates a new linked item.

Administrative-task ownership states are `proposed`, `accepted`, `in_progress`, `overdue`, `declined`, and `reassigned`. Every task has an owner, due date or explicit no-date status, reminder policy, and completion evidence. Ownership `accepted` activates the care item; ownership completion moves the care item to `completed`; a `cancelled`, `superseded`, or `completed` care item cannot remain `in_progress` or `overdue`; reassignment returns ownership to `proposed` for the new owner without changing clinical content.

### Medication Representation and Verification

Each medicine records exact source text, normalized display text only when confidently transcribed, strength, route, frequency, duration, schedule type, source, extraction confidence, family confirmation, and operational state.

Supported schedule types are fixed-time, interval, alternate-day, finite course, taper explicitly copied from the source, and PRN/as-needed explicitly copied from the source. Ayukiz never invents timing for a PRN medicine or simplifies a taper.

Medicines use the canonical care-item statuses above. Dose-event states are `confirmed_taken`, `confirmed_not_taken`, `no_response`, and `unknown`. No response is never converted into a missed dose without confirmation.

For every medicine, the operator performs a source comparison for name, strength, route, frequency, duration, and stop/start wording. The family then confirms the transcription. The operator verifies text, not clinical correctness. Brand/generic equivalence, substitutions, duplicate therapy, interactions, dosage safety, and treatment appropriateness require a qualified pharmacist or clinician.

For restrictions, tests, follow-ups, warning instructions, and general tasks, the operator compares exact wording, responsible person, date or timing, duration if present, and any explicit completion condition against the source. Missing details remain missing; the operator does not infer them. The family confirms the transcription before activation, and any later source change creates a linked superseding version.

### Pilot Automation Boundary

- **AI-assisted:** extraction drafts, summaries, factual question drafts, task suggestions, and classification into administrative, clinical, symptom-containing, or unknown content.
- **Operator-authorized:** every care-state activation, reminder schedule, message template, caregiver exception policy, packet delivery, suspension, closure, and deletion action. A previously approved fixed template may send on its activated schedule without per-message review.
- **Clinician-owned:** diagnosis, report interpretation, personalized urgency, treatment selection, medication change, dose safety, interactions, and resolution of disputed clinical instructions.
- **Not offered during the first five pilots:** model-generated autonomous messaging, continuous monitoring, device-based vital monitoring, autonomous symptom triage, automatic clinician messaging, or a promise that every message was delivered.

### Post-Visit Mode

1. Family forwards the prescription, discharge note, or doctor instructions.
2. Ayukiz extracts medicines, restrictions, follow-up date, tests, and explicit warning instructions.
3. The family confirms unclear text and whether the extracted schedule matches what the doctor said.
4. Ayukiz assigns each action to the parent, remote child, local family member, or doctor.
5. WhatsApp sends simple reminders and captures confirmations.
6. The remote child receives exceptions and a weekly summary rather than every routine message.

Ayukiz does not invent missing instructions. It asks the family to clarify them with the doctor.

### Second-Opinion Mode

1. Ayukiz assembles the current care state, relevant records, prior opinions, medicines, and unresolved questions.
2. The packet distinguishes documented facts, family-reported facts, unclear data, and questions.
3. After the consultation, the family adds the new doctor's documented opinion and instructions.
4. Ayukiz compares explicit statements and highlights unresolved differences such as contradictory follow-up timing or unclear medicine continuation.
5. The family asks a qualified clinician to resolve clinical disagreements.

"Reconciliation" means maintaining a clear decision trail and ensuring that unresolved issues have an owner. It does not mean AI selects the medically correct opinion.

### Between-Visit Elder-Care Mode

1. The parent receives one plain-language WhatsApp or voice prompt at a time.
2. A confirmation updates the shared care state.
3. A non-response first triggers a gentle retry, then a caregiver exception according to the confirmed family policy. It remains `no_response`, not a clinically interpreted missed dose.
4. Free-text symptoms do not trigger AI diagnosis. Conservative safety wording and the family's care contacts are shown.
5. The remote child sees outstanding actions and exceptions without needing repeated check-in calls.

Pilot interaction is limited to English and Hindi text plus WhatsApp voice notes. Every voice-note transcription is shown back for confirmation before it changes the care state. Regional languages, live voice calls, shared-phone privacy, vision limitations, and caregiver-only entry are assessed during onboarding; unsupported interaction moves responsibility to the caregiver rather than silently lowering quality.

### Reminder and Contact Policy

During onboarding, the family selects quiet hours, time zone, reminder recipients, retry permission, and caregiver escalation order. If fixed-template automation passes synthetic testing, the pilot default is one prompt at the family-confirmed time, one retry after 30 minutes, and a caregiver notification after 90 minutes. The operator activates the complete schedule in advance. If automation is unavailable, prompt times are restricted so all manual retries finish during staffed hours and no timer guarantee is offered. These timings coordinate acknowledgement only; they do not redefine the medically prescribed dosing window.

The service operates 09:00-20:00 India Standard Time during the pilot. If a fixed after-hours receipt template passes synthetic testing, it may respond automatically with the notice that Ayukiz is not continuously monitored and direct urgent concerns to the family's verified clinician, emergency contact, local emergency service, or nearest hospital. Otherwise, this warning is pinned in the conversation and no immediate response is promised. If the parent and caregiver are unreachable, the operator records the `unsafe_or_unreachable` suspension reason, sends the final permitted contact attempt during staffed hours, and suspends routine coordination. Ayukiz does not claim that emergency help has been dispatched.

The family verifies the parent phone, caregiver phone, treating-clinician contact, local emergency contact, and city during onboarding. The operator re-verifies these when a number changes. Ayukiz does not infer location from messages.

A deterministic high-priority trigger adds a banner to the operator queue and sends the caregiver a fixed safety template if that template is approved and delivery is available. It never contacts emergency services, changes the clinical wording, or bypasses the instruction to seek professional help. Outside staffed hours, only the tested fixed template can run.

### Clinical Handoff Protocol

When a clinical exception occurs, Ayukiz creates a handoff containing patient-consented identity, the exact family question, relevant attributed records, active and disputed instructions, and the reason the issue requires a clinician. The family chooses the recipient and, during the pilot, sends or explicitly authorizes the operator to send it through the approved channel.

A handoff is `sent` only when transmission is recorded, `acknowledged` only when the recipient confirms receipt, `answered` only when an attributed response is captured, and `closed` only when the family confirms the next action or accepts that the issue remains unresolved. If the clinician does not acknowledge within the family-agreed interval, ownership returns to the caregiver with alternate paths: contact the clinician directly, choose another qualified clinician, or seek emergency care if urgent. Ayukiz never converts silence into clinical reassurance.

### Clinician-on-Exception Boundary

No additional clinician is needed to extract literal instructions, schedule reminders, record confirmations, assemble records, display attributed opinions, or prepare factual questions.

Clinical accountability is required to choose between doctors, interpret symptoms or reports, recommend or change treatment, assess dosage or interaction safety, or provide personalized urgency decisions. The pilot must record how often these exceptions occur, who handled them, turnaround time, and whether the family already had an appropriate clinician.

Every inbound free-text message enters the authoritative inbound register as `received`, `draft_classified`, `operator_verified`, `actioned`, or `unprocessed_due_to_technical_failure`. AI classification is always a draft. During staffed hours, the operator reviews the queue before any model-generated response or care-state change; symptom-containing and unknown drafts receive highest priority. While review is pending, only a tested fixed receipt or safety template may send.

During the first five pilots, a qualified reviewer retrospectively audits 100% of registered inbound messages, including those classified administrative, to detect false negatives and refine the boundary. This is a separately budgeted pilot safety/research cost with a written rubric, reviewer confidentiality agreement, target review interval, and correction protocol. If the audit finds a material classification error, Ayukiz corrects the care state, records the incident, and follows the reviewed family-notification and clinical-escalation procedure. This audit does not authorize Ayukiz to provide clinical care.

## Trust and Failure States

- **Missing consent:** stop processing and request consent.
- **Unreadable document:** mark fields unclear; never guess.
- **Conflicting sources:** show both sources and request family or clinician clarification.
- **Parent does not respond:** apply the family-selected retry/escalation rule; do not label the medicine as missed without confirmation.
- **Any symptom free text:** display conservative safety routing; the deterministic keyword policy may add a high-priority banner but never a severity judgment.
- **Reviewer unavailable:** state that the clinical question remains unresolved and direct the family to its treating clinician.
- **Message delivery uncertainty:** expose the visible WhatsApp status to the caregiver; do not promise delivery detection or treat a sent message as delivered or confirmed.
- **Care-plan change:** preserve the previous instruction, source the new one, and require confirmation before changing active reminders.
- **Consent or permission withdrawn:** suspend immediately, revoke access, stop messages, offer export, and start the approved deletion procedure.
- **Operator, clinician, or family terminates participation:** suspend routine work, provide a final factual summary and unresolved-items list, return ownership to the family, offer export, and process retention or deletion choice.
- **Unsafe use, nonpayment, or repeated inability to contact:** suspend service rather than allowing an apparently active but unmanaged care state.
- **Security incident:** stop affected processing, preserve only the evidence allowed by the incident policy, notify the designated owner, and follow the reviewed breach-response and family-notification procedure.

### Minimum Data-Handling Specification

Before launch, the chosen pilot stack must document data flow from WhatsApp intake through operator device, restricted storage, reviewer transmission, exports, backups, and deletion. It must name the data owner, approved operators, vendor access, storage region if known, encryption controls, authentication, sharing defaults, backup behaviour, retention clock, deletion verification, and incident contact.

Real case folders are segregated by family with least-privilege access. Public links, personal cloud drives, analytics capture, model training, screenshots in issue trackers, and real records in git are prohibited. Operator devices may not retain downloaded media beyond the documented processing window. A weekly reconciliation checks the message log, care state, pending tasks, unresolved handoffs, access list, and deletion queue.

The authoritative inbound register is an append-only pilot log populated from the dedicated WhatsApp channel and reconciled against the visible conversation at the start and end of each staffed day. Deleted-before-capture messages, inaccessible voice notes, device failures, and export gaps are recorded as `unprocessed_due_to_technical_failure`; they remain in the denominator when a notification or visible trace exists. The design does not claim knowledge of messages that never reached the approved channel.

Ayukiz-controlled copies and recipient-controlled copies have different retention boundaries. The consent disclosure states that a family member, treating clinician, or reviewer who receives an authorized packet may retain a copy outside Ayukiz's control. Every export records recipient, purpose, authorization, transfer method, timestamp, and document version. Approved transfer methods and password/key exchange are set by the launch-gate review. Deletion verification covers Ayukiz-controlled storage; the family receives instructions for requesting deletion from external recipients, but Ayukiz does not falsely claim it can erase those copies.

## Success Criteria

For the first five 30-day paid pilots:

- At least three families prepay rather than accepting a free trial.
- Every family uses the required core: post-visit care-state creation plus between-visit follow-through. Second-opinion mode is available only when naturally triggered and is measured separately rather than forced into every case.
- At least four families complete a post-visit care state with every active item carrying provenance, confirmation status, and no unresolved contradiction governing the reminder.
- At least three remote children identify a specific call, coordination task, or planned trip they did not make because the same objective was completed through Ayukiz.
- At least three families want to renew at the end of 30 days.
- One hundred percent of messages registered on the approved inbound channel receive an event-log outcome, including technical failures; every item tagged clinical, symptom-containing, or unknown is routed according to policy without unreviewed diagnosis, treatment selection, medicine change, or reassurance.
- Zero real patient records enter git, public links, demonstrations, training data, or analytics.

Measurement definitions are fixed before enrollment:

- A `care_action` is a timestamped task with mode, owner, due state, completion evidence, and source.
- A `clinical_exception` is any request or content requiring diagnosis, interpretation, personalized urgency, treatment selection, medicine change, dosage or interaction judgment, or resolution of disputed clinical instructions.
- `routed` means the family received the handoff and recipient options; `acknowledged` and `answered` are recorded separately and never inferred.
- A `replaced activity` requires the caregiver to name the intended call, task, or trip, its purpose, and how Ayukiz completed the same non-clinical objective.
- Cost-to-serve records operator minutes by activity, reviewer cost, messaging cost, and unresolved-case cost for every family.
- Denominators are all enrolled families, all inbound messages, all care actions, and all clinical exceptions; failed and suspended cases remain in the denominator.
- Day-0 and day-30 questions are fixed before enrollment: coordination hours in the prior month, care-related trips, confidence about the next action and owner, willingness to renew, and acceptable renewal price.

## Distribution Plan

- Acquisition: direct outreach to remote adult children already travelling or coordinating care for an elder parent.
- Sale: a prepaid 30-day concierge pilot for one parent and one primary caregiver. ₹499 is a willingness-to-pay experiment, not a sustainable price claim. It excludes clinician fees, caps onboarding and routine operator work in the written pilot offer, and measures actual cost-to-serve before renewal pricing.
- Intake and interaction: a dedicated manually operated WhatsApp Business account plus a short consent and setup call, subject to the launch-gate review. Voice means WhatsApp voice notes, not live clinical calls.
- Delivery: WhatsApp care prompts, caregiver summaries, and PDF second-opinion packs when needed.
- Operations: manual case state outside git using a restricted storage location chosen before the first non-family record.
- Clinical exceptions: use the family's existing treating clinician first. If there is no response, the family owns direct follow-up or selection of another qualified clinician. Contract a paid Ayukiz reviewer only for an actual exception, separately consented and priced, after legal review permits the workflow.
- Software distribution is deferred. If repeated paid use is proven, the next step is a caregiver web dashboard with WhatsApp/voice parent interaction, not an elder-facing app.
